GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Have another question? We’re happy to help.

Contact us →
How do I book an appointment?

Use the request form or call the clinic. A staff member confirms the appointment and available time.

Do you accept walk-ins?

Availability varies. Please call ahead so the team can advise you before you visit.

What should I bring to my visit?

Bring a photo ID, insurance information if applicable, a medication list and any relevant records.

Do you offer telehealth?

Some visits may be suitable for virtual care. Ask the front desk about options when booking.

What if I need urgent help?

This website is not monitored for emergencies. Call your local emergency number or visit the nearest emergency department.
